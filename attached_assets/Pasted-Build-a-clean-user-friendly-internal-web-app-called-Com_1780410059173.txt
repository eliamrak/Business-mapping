Build a clean, user-friendly internal web app called “Compensation Strategy Dashboard.”

Do not use AI features in this first version. This app should be a calculator, scenario builder, and PDF export tool for modeling clinician compensation, business goals, and long-term practice planning.

The app should be simple, intuitive, and easy to navigate. The user should be able to create business goals, build compensation scenarios, assign different compensation models to different clinicians inside a scenario, compare scenarios side by side, and export clean PDF summaries.

Core Purpose

The app should help a group practice owner answer:

1. What does the business need financially?
2. What does each clinician need to produce?
3. How do different compensation models affect clinician compensation?
4. How do W2 and 1099 classifications affect tax burden and business net?
5. Which offers meet the business goals while still being attractive to clinicians?
6. What is the overall business impact if different clinicians are on different pay structures?

1. Business Goals

5. Scenario Comparison
6. Clinician Impact View
7. PDF Export

Use a clean dashboard-style interface. Avoid clutter. Each tab should answer one clear question.

Tab 1: Business Goals

Purpose:
Help the user define financial goals for the business over time.

Fields:

- Time Horizon: 1 year, 3 year, 5 year, 10 year
- Owner Pay Goal
- Second Owner Pay Goal
- Annual Overhead Goal

- Growth Fund Goal

- Desired Owner Clinical Caseload
- Notes

Calculated Outputs:
- Total Annual Business Need
- Required Net Per Clinician

Formula:
Total Annual Business Need =
Owner Pay Goal
+ Second Owner Pay Goal
+ Annual Overhead Goal
+ Business Profit Goal
+ Building Fund Goal
+ Emergency Reserve Goal
+ Growth Fund Goal

Required Net Per Clinician =
Total Annual Business Need / Desired Number of Clinicians

Allow the user to create, edit, duplicate, and delete business goal profiles.

Calculation Requirements

Annual Sessions =
Sessions Per Week * Weeks Worked Per Year

Annual Production =
Annual Sessions * Session Rate

Practice Revenue Before Cap =
Session Rate * Practice Pre-Cap Split

Sessions To Cap =
Cap Amount / Practice Revenue Per Session Before Cap

If cap is off:
Use same split for all sessions.

If cap is on:
Pre-Cap Sessions =
MIN(Annual Sessions, Sessions To Cap)

Post-Cap Sessions =
MAX(0, Annual Sessions - Sessions To Cap)

Clinician Compensation =
(Pre-Cap Sessions * Session Rate * Pre-Cap Clinician Split)
+
(Post-Cap Sessions * Session Rate * Post-Cap Clinician Split)

Practice Gross Revenue =
(Pre-Cap Sessions * Session Rate * Pre-Cap Practice Split)
+
(Post-Cap Sessions * Session Rate * Post-Cap Practice Split)

Employer Obligations Paid By Practice =
If W2:
Clinician Compensation * Total Employer Burden %
If 1099:
0

Total Employer Burden % =
W2 Employer FICA %
+ FUTA/SUTA %
+ Workers Comp %
+ Other Employer Burden %

Practice Net Before General Practice Overhead =
Practice Gross Revenue - Employer Obligations Paid By Practice

Clinician Payroll Tax Estimate:
For W2:
Clinician Compensation * Employee FICA %
For 1099:
Clinician Compensation * Self Employment Tax %
Default Employee FICA = 7.65%
Default Self Employment Tax = 15.3%

Estimated Compensation After Payroll Taxes =
Clinician Compensation - Clinician Payroll Tax Estimate

Important:
This is not full take-home pay. Do not include federal income tax, state income tax, deductions, retirement, or itemized tax planning unless added later. Label this as “After Payroll Taxes Only.”

UX Requirements

The app should be extremely user-friendly.

Design principles:
- One screen answers one question
- Use cards instead of dense spreadsheets
- Use clear labels
- Use tooltips for confusing concepts
- Use green, yellow, and red indicators
- Avoid clutter
- Keep clinician-facing views clean
- Keep owner-only views separate

Required tooltips:
- Annual Production: total revenue generated before compensation split
- Clinician Compensation: amount paid to clinician before income taxes
- Employer Obligations: employment-related costs paid by the practice for W2 clinicians
- Practice Net: practice revenue after clinician compensation and employer obligations, before general overhead
- Payroll Tax Estimate: estimate of payroll taxes only, not total tax liability
- Cap: amount the practice receives before post-cap split begins

Default Assumptions

Use these defaults:
- Session Rate: $175
- Sessions Per Week: 20
- Weeks Worked Per Year: 48
- Cap: $50,000
- W2 Employer FICA: 7.65%
- FUTA/SUTA: 1.0%
- Workers Comp: 0.5%
- Other Employer Burden: 0%
- Employee FICA: 7.65%
- Self Employment Tax: 15.3%
- Default 1099 model: 60/40 pre-cap, 95/5 post-cap
- Default W2 model: 60/40 pre-cap, 75/25 post-cap

Important Conceptual Requirement

The app must separate:
1. Compensation formula
2. Employment classification
3. Tax responsibility
4. Practice net

Do not make the mistake of treating W2 and 1099 compensation as automatically different.

The app should allow compensation to be the same or different depending on the scenario inputs.

The user should be able to demonstrate:
- Same production
- Same compensation
- Different payroll tax responsibility
- Different practice employer obligations
- Different practice net
